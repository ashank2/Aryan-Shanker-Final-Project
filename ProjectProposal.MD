## 1. The Big Idea:

Evora, is an AI-powered personal transportation assistant designed to help users develop and adopt more sustainable transportation habits. At the end of the day, users will input their methods of transportation (ex: biking, forms of public transport taken, carpooling), the corresponding distances, activities attributed to them and the date. Once done, the user's transportation choices will be logged, and the Evora AI will help to analyze the user's daily carbon emissions, provide actionable recommendations and aid in route optimization. As more personal data is inputted by the user, habit analyses will be conducted, creating respective user carbon emissions graphs, offering key insights on a weekly basis to users to enable them to reduce their own carbon footprint, increase efficiency, and commute more sustainably. 

### Topics Explored:
* Sustainable Transportation Practices - exploring more carbon-efficient routes and aiding in the ease of transition amongst users towards more sustainable processes of transportation on a daily basis. 
* Leveraging of AI techniques for route optimization and habit analysis. Thus, allowing for: Carbon footprint tracking and reduction strategies. Providing the building blocks in terms of habit formation for other realms of personal life to be more sustainable amongst users.  

### Minimum Viable Product:
* Users input daily transportation habits, and corresponding distances, labelled with the activity name and date: (ex: Activity Name: Work Commute, Mode Of Transportation: Biking, Distance Travelled: 10 Kilometers, Date: April 12th, 2025).
* As a result, the personal data inputted by the user will be logged and displayed.
* Leading to the Evora AI analyzing daily carbon emissions, provide recommendations for greener alternatives and feedback on daily routes taken. With user data being visualized and presented day by day per user. 

### Stretch Goal:
* Integrate AI algorithms to suggest optimized routes based on carbon efficiency and user preference.
* Include real-time data integration with APIs (for example: Carbon Intensity API) to calculate emissions more accurately. 
Carbon Intensity API Website: https://api.carbonintensity.org.uk/
